# Everyday Supermarket Sales Dashboard

## Project Overview
An Excel-based data analytics project focused on analyzing retail sales performance, profitability, product categories, customer segments, and regional trends to support data-driven business decisions.

## Dataset used
-<a href="https://github.com/Nwadiarugideon/DATA-ANALYSIS-DASHBOARD/blob/main/Everyday%20Supermarket%20Dashboard%20(version%202).xlsb.xlsx">Dataset</a>
## Tools Used
- Microsoft Excel
- PivotTables and data visualization
- KPI analysis and dashboard reporting

## Key Analysis
- Sales and profit performance
- Product category analysis
- Customer segment analysis
- Regional sales performance
- Key business performance indicators (KPIs)

## Objective
To transform raw sales data into meaningful insights that help identify business trends and support informed decision-making.

## Business Questions
1. Which product category generates the highest revenue?
2. Who are the top 7 customers by sales revenue?
3. Which customer segment contributes the most to total sales?
4. Which shipping mode is used most frequently?
5. Which sales representative generates the highest revenue?
6. How do regional sales change over time?
7. Which region generates the highest total revenue?
8. How is total revenue distributed across the four regions?
9. What is the total revenue generated by the business?
10. What is the total quantity of products sold?

## Data Analysis Process

1. **Data Quality Assessment:** Inspected the dataset for missing values, duplicate records, inconsistencies, and anomalies.
2. **Data Cleaning:** Addressed identified data quality issues and ensured consistency across relevant fields.
3. **Exploratory Data Analysis (EDA):** Examined sales, profit, quantity, discounts, product categories, customer segments, and regional performance.
4. **KPI Development:** Identified key performance indicators to evaluate business performance.
5. **Data Aggregation:** Used Excel PivotTables to summarize and compare sales metrics across different business dimensions.
6. **Dashboard Development:** Created interactive visualizations, KPI summaries, and regional filters to communicate findings effectively.
7. **Business Insights:** Analyzed trends and performance to identify top customers, sales representatives, product categories, and regions.

## Project Insights
- **Revenue:** Generated approximately $2.30 million in total sales.
- **Top Category:** Technology recorded the highest sales.
- **Customer Segment:** Consumer customers contributed the largest share of revenue.
- **Regional Performance:** The West region recorded the highest sales.
- **Shipping:** Standard Class was the most frequently used shipping method.
- **Profitability:** Total profit was approximately $286,397.
